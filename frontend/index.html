ABOUT AASRA

From raw data to meaningful disaster intelligence.

AASRA is a disaster-management platform concept focused on bringing environmental data, disaster monitoring, risk assessment, machine learning and satellite intelligence together.

The platform is being developed in stages so that every major feature can be tested with real data before being used in the final system.
